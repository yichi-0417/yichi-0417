# Above the Noise: Think Deep. Build Long.

Yichi is an architect and independent researcher working across enterprise cloud modernization, hybrid cloud architecture, AI transformation, and applied AI solutions.

Beyond professional practice, her research explores how people, technologies, and complex systems evolve together. 
At the center of this work is a broader interest in architecture as a discipline: how problems are framed, how decisions are made, how observations evolve into working hypotheses and methodologies, and how knowledge can be designed for long-term reuse.

Current research and writing focus on three interconnected areas:

- **Japan Cloud Lift**: Enterprise cloud modernization from an architect’s perspective, covering networking, security, governance, compute, and data.
- **AI Architecting**: Methods, workflows, and architecture patterns for designing AI systems through the lens of design research.
- **Thinking in Architecture**: Essays and working hypotheses on architectural reasoning, system behavior, representation alignment, and the relationship between human intent and technical outcomes.

This GitHub serves as an open research notebook where ideas are documented, challenged, connected, refined, and shared through writing and practice.

Thoughtful discussions and research collaborations are always welcome.
